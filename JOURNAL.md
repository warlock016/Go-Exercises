# Learning Journal

This journal is your space for reflection and growth. After each learning session, spend 5-10 minutes capturing your thoughts. Research shows that reflection significantly improves retention and understanding.

---

## How to Use This Journal

**Daily Entries** (after each 4-hour learning block):
- What did you learn today?
- What was challenging? Why?
- What "clicked" or made sense?
- How would you explain this concept to someone else?
- What will you review tomorrow?

**Weekly Reflections** (end of week):
- What was your biggest breakthrough this week?
- What concept still feels unclear?
- How has your understanding evolved?
- What will you focus on next week?
